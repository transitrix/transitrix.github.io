Publishing

Where Transitrix library sheets, explainers, and releases are carried outward. One statement per channel, stated once so a week that publishes nothing is then a visible decision.

Background

The channels above are the only two Transitrix publishes to. Channel bios, paid distribution, and cross-posting arrangements are decided elsewhere and stated on the channels themselves, not here.

The statement above answers a single question: "Given a new library sheet or explainer (or a release), where does it go and when?" A week with no publication is then legible — not noise, but a data point against the stated cadence.
