Transitrix Methodology

Keep the goals, processes, capabilities and systems behind a decision in a versioned architecture repository.

Methodology defines what goes in the model and how its records relate. Transitrix Studio opens and previews models written with those rules.

What your team maintains

Write structured text for things your team needs to track, such as a goal, a process step or an application. Link those records so readers can trace why a change is planned and what it affects. Keep source evidence, modelling rules and accepted facts in their own places. Changes can then be reviewed in version control.

Worked example: Acme’s expansion

In the fictional Acme model, market pressure and competition connect to a goal of launching in three EU markets. The model records planned changes to customer systems, data protection and onboarding. The worked example presents those records alongside the capabilities and systems involved.

Start with one question

Choose a decision or process your team already needs to explain. Record the relevant facts, connect them and review the result together. Follow the getting-started guide or open the model examples to see the file formats directly.
